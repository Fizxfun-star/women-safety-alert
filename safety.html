QUICK ACTION GUIDE

If you feel unsafe,
trust your instincts.

Use these simple steps to move toward a safer situation and contact someone you trust.

01

Move to a public place

Head toward a shop, security desk, reception area, police station, or another busy location.

02

Contact someone you trust

Call or message your emergency contact and share your current location.

03

Use the SOS alert

Return to the home screen and hold the SOS button to prepare your emergency message.

04

Call emergency services

In India, 112 is the national emergency number. Use it when immediate assistance is required.
